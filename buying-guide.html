How to buy a garment printing machine in India

A buyer's guide to comparing DTF, TPU label, embroidery and flocking quotes, written by a supplier who will tell you which machine not to buy.

By Sanjeev Budhiraja, Founder, Axis Enterprises. Published 15 August 2026. Last updated 15 August 2026. 7 min read.

What should you check before buying a garment printing machine?

Check 7 things, in this order: the supplier's engineer response time to your address, whether print heads and spares are stocked in India, who supplies consumables after month 3, the exact print head model and count, the real output at the pass setting you will run rather than peak speed, the power and floor space the machine needs, and whether the supplier will print your own design on your own material before you release the balance payment. Price is the 8th question, not the 1st. A garment printing machine has to earn for 5 to 10 years, so the total of machine plus 5 years of support is the number that matters.

The 7 checks

What are the 7 questions to ask before you sign?

Ask each one in writing. A supplier who cannot answer 1 to 3 in writing has told you what their after-sales looks like.

1. How many hours to an engineer at my address?

This is the first question because it is the one that decides whether the machine earns. Ask for the number of hours and the number of engineers in your state. Our own commitment is 24 to 48 hours, same day or next day in metro and tier 1 cities, up to 3 days for remote locations, from a service team of 48 people across 11 cities.

2. Is a replacement print head in stock in India?

A print head that ships from China after failure costs you 2 to 4 weeks of production, not just the price of the head. Ask which heads are held in India, at which location, and what the replacement lead time is once the fault is confirmed.

3. Who supplies ink, film and powder after month 3?

Consumables are where a cheap machine gets expensive. Confirm the supply source, the price per unit today, and whether the machine will run on third-party consumables or only on the supplier's own.

4. What is the exact print head model and count?

Head model and quantity set both your speed and your repair bill. "Industrial head" is not an answer. A 600 mm DTF line running 6 i3200 A1 heads is a different machine from a 4-head unit of the same width, and it should be quoted as such.

5. What is the output at the pass setting I will actually run?

Peak speed is quoted at the fastest pass, which is rarely the quality setting you sell at. As an example, a 6-head 600 mm DTF printer runs about 27 sqm per hour at 4 pass, 23 at 6 pass, and 15 at 8 pass. Plan your capacity on the pass you will use, not the number on the brochure.

6. What power point and floor area does it need?

Arrange this before the machine ships. A 600 mm DTF printer draws about 2200W at 10A, while its automatic shaker draws about 7000W at 35A on 220V. The printer and shaker together need roughly 4.5 metres of run length. Most installation delays in India are electrical, not mechanical.

7. Will you print my design on my material before I pay the balance?

Sampling is the only test that survives contact with your order book. Ask the supplier to install, then run your own artwork on your own garment before handover. If a supplier resists this, the brochure is doing work the machine cannot.

Straight answers

Buying questions, answered directly

How much does a garment printing machine cost in India?

Across the Axis garment printing range, machines run from 3 lakh to 16 lakh. A single-head A3 label printer sits at the bottom of that band and a 600 mm multi-head DTF line with an automatic shaker sits at the top. Price is driven by print width, print head count and whether a shaker or curing unit is included. Ask any seller to quote the model name and head model, not a category.

What is the difference between DTF and DTG printing?

DTF prints onto a PET film that is powdered, cured and heat pressed onto the garment, so it works on cotton, polyester, denim, nylon and leather. DTG prints ink directly into the fabric and works best on high-cotton garments. For a mixed order book across fabric types, DTF covers more of your catalogue with one machine.

How many people are needed to run a DTF line?

One trained operator runs the printer and shaker line. A second person on the heat press keeps the output moving. Our engineer trains your operator during installation and the operator stays in a WhatsApp group afterwards for daily checks.

What power supply does a DTF printer need?

A 600 mm DTF printer typically draws about 2200W at 10A, and the automatic shaker draws about 7000W at 35A on 220V. Confirm the electrical point and the floor space before the machine ships, because arranging it after delivery is what delays most installations.

What consumables will I buy every month?

PET film, hot melt powder and pigment ink in CMYK plus white for DTF. UV ink for TPU label printing. Ask your supplier to confirm in writing that all of these, plus replacement print heads, are stocked in India.

Should I buy the cheapest machine available?

No. A garment printing machine has to earn for 5 to 10 years. The cheapest machine usually carries the thinnest margin for the seller, which means there is no money left to service it after the sale. Judge the total of machine plus 5 years of support, not the invoice.
