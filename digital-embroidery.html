What is digital embroidery used for?

Digital embroidery stitches a digitised design onto fabric using a computer-controlled multi-needle machine. It is used for school and corporate uniforms, caps, jackets, backpacks and canvas bags, where thick or curved material holds stitch better than a flat print and where the same logo repeats for years. The artwork is digitised once and the stitch file runs unchanged after that, so the setup cost is paid a single time. Embroidery questions

What is digital embroidery?

Digital embroidery stitches a digitised design onto fabric with a computer-controlled multi-needle machine. The artwork is converted into a stitch file once, then the same design runs for years without further setup, which is why it suits uniforms, caps and repeat logo work.

Which products suit embroidery rather than printing?

Caps, jackets, uniforms, backpacks and canvas bags. Thick and curved materials hold stitch better than a flat print, and a stitched logo is what lets a garment carry a higher price than a printed one.

What does an embroidery machine cost?

Cost moves with head count, needle count and stitch area. A single-head unit for sampling and a 12-head unit for production are 2 different businesses. Send us your daily output target and stitch area and we quote the exact model rather than a range.
