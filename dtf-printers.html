What is DTF printing?

DTF, or direct to film, is a garment printing process where the design is printed in CMYK plus white onto a PET film, coated with hot melt powder, cured, and then heat pressed onto the garment. Because there is no screen and no plate, a 1-piece order costs the same to set up as a 100-piece order. DTF works on cotton, polyester, denim, nylon, leather and canvas, which is why a unit with a mixed catalogue can cover most of it with one machine. The global direct to film printing market was valued at USD 2.7 billion in 2024 and is forecast to reach USD 3.9 billion by 2030, according to Grand View Research.

How DTF works

What are the 4 steps from artwork to garment?

STEP 1

Print on film

CMYK first, white behind it, straight onto PET film at 2400 DPI. No screen, no plate, no setup cost.

STEP 2

Powder

The shaker lays hot melt powder on the wet ink and returns the excess automatically, so powder does not sit where it should not.

STEP 3

Cure

Preheating, intermediate heating and after heating in one pass. The film comes out dry and ready to store or press.

STEP 4

Press

Heat press onto the garment and peel. Works on cotton, polyester, denim, nylon, leather, canvas and swimwear fabric.

Straight answers

DTF questions we get every week

How does a DTF printer work?

A DTF printer prints the design in CMYK with a white layer behind it onto a PET film. The film passes through a shaker that applies hot melt powder and returns the excess, then through a curing unit. The finished film is heat pressed onto the garment and peeled. The whole process needs no screen, no plate and no minimum order quantity.

What fabrics can a DTF transfer be applied to?

Cotton, polyester, denim, nylon, leather, canvas, chemical fibre, swimsuit fabric, diving suit material and PVC all accept a DTF transfer. That range is the main reason a garment unit with a mixed catalogue buys DTF rather than a fabric-specific process.

How fast is a 6-head 600 mm DTF printer?

The Nexus 600 6H runs about 27 square metres per hour at 4 pass, 23 at 6 pass, and 15 at 8 pass, at 2400 DPI. Plan your daily capacity on the pass setting you sell at, not on the peak figure.

What consumables does a DTF printer need?

PET film, hot melt powder, and pigment ink in CMYK plus white. All 3 stay in stock with us in India, along with replacement print heads and spare parts.

How many operators does a DTF line need?

One trained operator runs the printer and shaker line. A second person on the heat press keeps output moving. Our engineer trains your operator during installation.

What power supply does the machine need?

The printer takes about 2200W at 10A on 220V or 110V. The automatic shaker takes about 7000W at 35A on 220V. Arrange both points before delivery so installation is not held up.
