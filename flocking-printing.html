What is flocking printing on garments?

Flocking printing applies short textile fibres onto an adhesive design on the garment, creating a raised velvet surface rather than a flat printed one. It is used on fashionwear, kidswear, statement t-shirts and sports numbering, where the tactile finish is what supports a higher retail price. Flocking sits alongside DTF rather than replacing it: DTF gives photographic colour with a smooth feel, flocking gives texture with simpler colour work. Straight answers

Flocking questions

What is flocking printing?

Flocking applies short fibres to an adhesive design on the fabric, producing a raised velvet surface the customer feels before reading the design. It is used on fashionwear, kidswear and sportswear numbering, where the finish is what justifies a higher retail price.

How is flocking different from DTF?

DTF gives photographic colour with a smooth hand feel. Flocking gives a textured, raised, velvet surface with simpler colour work. They sell to different price points, and many units run both.

Does flocking survive washing?

A correctly applied flock design holds through repeated wash when the adhesive, temperature and press time are right for the fabric. Those 3 variables are what your operator is trained on at installation.

What do you need from me to quote a flocking line?

Fabric type, daily output in pieces, the largest flocked area on the garment, and your city. Those 4 answers decide the machine and the installation timeline.
