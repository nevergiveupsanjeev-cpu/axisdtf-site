What is TPU label printing?

TPU label printing prints a brand label or badge directly onto TPU or PU material using UV ink in CMYK plus white and varnish, with adjustable ink thickness that produces a flat or a raised 3D label. It replaces the plate-based label production most garment and footwear brands outsource today. The A3 machine Axis supplies prints at 2400 DPI on a 300 mm width, runs at 1.8 square metres per hour at 8 pass, and needs only a 220V 700W single phase point. Typical output is labels and badges for shoes, bags, jackets, kidswear and accessories.

Straight answers

Label printing questions

What is a TPU label printer?

A TPU label printer prints CMYK plus white and varnish directly onto TPU or PU material to make raised, flexible brand labels and badges for shoes, bags and garments. Because the design comes straight from the file, there is no plate cost and no die cost when the design or colour changes.

How fast is the A3 UV transfer label printer?

The A3 UV Transfer Label DTF Printer runs 1.8 square metres per hour at 8 pass with a single HD 3200 head at 2400 DPI. On small labels that is a large number of pieces per hour, and the machine runs unattended while your operator does something else.

Can it print a raised 3D label?

Yes. Ink thickness is adjustable, so the same machine produces a flat label or a raised embossed one. That single control is what lets one machine serve 2 different product lines.

What material does it print on?

TPU and PU. Colour configuration is CMYK plus white plus varnish, running on UV ink with a 450 ml capacity.

What power supply does it need?

220V, 50/60Hz, 700W. That is an ordinary single phase point, so the machine fits into the corner of an existing unit without an electrical upgrade.

Why print labels in house instead of buying them?

Because a label vendor sets your delivery date and your minimum quantity. In house, a sample costs you ink and 10 minutes instead of a vendor order and a week, and a design change costs nothing because there is no plate to remake.
